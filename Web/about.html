About Gujju Tools

MyTools is a free online platform that provides simple utilities to help users work with documents, images and everyday digital tasks. Our goal is to make useful tools accessible to everyone without requiring downloads or technical knowledge.

Our Mission

We believe software should be fast, simple and accessible. Instead of installing heavy programs, you can complete tasks instantly in your browser — from merging PDFs to generating secure passwords.

Your Privacy Matters

We do not permanently store your files. Uploaded files are processed automatically and removed after completion. We do not read, share or sell user data.

Security

All tools run through automated processing systems. No human views your uploaded documents. Sensitive files remain private during processing.

Free to Use

Most tools are available without login or subscription. Our aim is to keep essential utilities free and easy to access.

Future Plans

We continuously add new tools such as converters, generators and productivity utilities to make MyTools a complete all-in-one toolkit.
